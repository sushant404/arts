ABOUT ANSUI RYUSHO
Ansui Ryusho is the pen name of Sushant Shrestha — venture capitalist, teacher, and musician based between Bellevue and Kathmandu. His creative practice is rooted in integral theory, metamodernism, and the Newar tantric lineages of the Kathmandu Valley. The work moves between lyric and liturgy, between the personal and the planetary, always seeking the place where they meet.
